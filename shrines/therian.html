Inspiration

I am a black phase wolf dog :) I feel most connected to the coastal wolves in British Columbia. They have a largely marine diet and they play in the sand. I am trying to be more comfortable saying I'm a "therian." I have had these experiences my whole life, and I have always kind of leaned on wolves or dogs in general to help explain my feelings and experiences. So, the the experience is old and the label is new. I will usually just tell people I'm a dog or wolf, in passing, and they kind of catch on enough. It really does explain a lot about how I process and experience things.

I experience mental, phantom, and dream shifts. A mental shift will usually make verbalizing pretty hard, and unless I can act on them, I end up feeling like a dog staring longingly out the window at the outside world, where he wants to frolick and howl. It feels awkward to walk around on two legs if I think about it too much, but that's easy to ignore if I need to be walking around.

I use the wolf to describe the more impulsive and touch-oriented of my main forms of emotional expression—the other being the swan, more analytical and verbal.
